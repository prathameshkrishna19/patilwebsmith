Healthcare Appointment System

Patient management and doctor appointment booking platform for clinics and hospitals.

Project Overview

A comprehensive healthcare management system enabling patients to book appointments online, track medical history, receive digital prescriptions, and communicate with doctors. Features include a doctor portal, appointment calendar, and admin dashboard for complete clinic management.

Key Features

✅ Online appointment booking
✅ Patient medical history records
✅ Doctor availability calendar
✅ Digital prescription system
✅ SMS & email notifications
✅ Admin dashboard & reports
✅ Multi-role user access (Patient, Doctor, Admin)
✅ Secure data storage & HIPAA compliance

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Managing simultaneous doctor appointments and preventing double bookings.
Solution: Implemented real-time availability calendar and instant booking confirmation.
Challenge: Tracking patient medical history securely.
Solution: Developed encrypted digital records accessible only to authorized users.
